I worked closely with clients to understand their vision and objectives, delivering custom solutions tailored to their needs while ensuring optimal performance and scalability.

In addition to web development, I managed and maintained LAMP (Linux, Apache, MySQL, PHP) servers, ensuring the smooth operation of hosting environments for various client websites and applications. My responsibilities included troubleshooting server issues, implementing security protocols, and performing regular backups and updates to ensure data integrity and availability. This role strengthened my expertise in server administration and the overall web hosting infrastructure, allowing me to provide clients with reliable and secure hosting services.

As part of my role, I also contributed to sales efforts by providing technical insights during client consultations, helping to drive business growth by aligning web solutions with client goals. I collaborated with the sales team to create tailored proposals and ensure the technical feasibility of projects, often bridging the gap between client expectations and development capabilities. Additionally, I oversaw the project management aspect of web development, coordinating timelines, resources, and team members to ensure successful project delivery within scope and budget.
