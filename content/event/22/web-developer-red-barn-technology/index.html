As a Web Developer at Red Barn Technology, I was responsible for maintaining and automating a range of software applications, with a focus on streamlining workflows and improving the efficiency of internal and client-facing systems. I collaborated with cross-functional teams to ensure the smooth operation of web applications used by enterprise clients, offering tailored solutions that met the unique needs of each business.

I played a key role in developing, maintaining, and integrating software solutions for web applications, ensuring that these systems functioned seamlessly and delivered high performance under varying loads. I utilized a range of technologies to ensure code quality, scalability, and security, while also prioritizing user experience and system stability. By integrating existing software solutions with new technologies, I helped Red Barn's enterprise clients optimize their operations, improve data accessibility, and achieve their business goals.

Additionally, I focused on automating processes to reduce manual intervention and increase the accuracy and efficiency of the software applications. This involved writing scripts, setting up scheduled tasks, and creating automated workflows to ensure that processes ran smoothly without the need for constant supervision.

My role also required close collaboration with other developers, project managers, and clients to align software solutions with client requirements and ensure that deliverables were met on time. I contributed to the successful development and deployment of a variety of web applications, making an impact on both internal operations and client satisfaction.
