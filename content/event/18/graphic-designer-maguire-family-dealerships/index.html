Maguire Family of Dealerships

As a Graphic Designer at Maguire Family of Dealerships, I played a key role in designing and producing a wide range of digital and print materials for the dealership’s marketing and branding efforts. I was responsible for delegating tasks and managing the design of high-quality graphic assets for promotional campaigns, including flyers, brochures, social media posts, advertisements, and banners. This role required a keen eye for detail and a strong understanding of branding principles to ensure that all design materials aligned with the dealership's identity and marketing objectives.

In addition to my design responsibilities, I also managed client relations by collaborating closely with the sales and marketing teams to understand client needs and deliver customized design solutions. I worked directly with clients to ensure their vision was accurately translated into visual designs that resonated with their target audience.

A significant part of my work involved creating custom graphics for automotive events, such as trade shows, sales promotions, and special dealership events. I designed event-specific branding, signage, decals, and materials that enhanced the event experience and promoted the dealership’s offerings. This opportunity allowed me to showcase my creativity while simultaneously improving event engagement and customer participation.

My leadership role in delegating design tasks and collaborating with other departments helped ensure the smooth flow of projects and timely delivery of all marketing materials. This position enhanced my skills in project management, client communication, and multi-tasking in a fast-paced environment, all while delivering exceptional design work.

Key Skills Developed:

  • Branding and Visual Identity
  • Client Relationship Management
  • Event Branding and Custom Graphics
  • Project Management and Task Delegation
  • Sales and Marketing Collaboration
  • Time Management and Multi-Tasking
